# v0.5.0 — SEO, category pages and performance

> **Search titles and page copy now lead with the words people search for, each product category has its own page, and the public pages load lighter.** **28 commits** since v0.4.0. 1 migration. The release adds category pages with a breadcrumb and a category menu, a plain-text site guide, form hints on the contact form and several performance changes. The live site stays out of search indexes until 1.0.0.

This release builds on the SEO foundation from v0.4.0. It rewrites the titles, descriptions and page copy, gives every category an address of its own, and makes the public site faster to load. The panel gets fields for the new category texts.

---

# Search and page copy
- Titles, descriptions and headings of the public pages start with the search words for the page. The brand name comes from the site template.
- The page copy of the home, about, vision, services, projects, references, catalogs and contact pages is rewritten in Turkish and English.
- The product pages build their descriptions from power, size, colour and material. A detail that is empty is left out.

# Category pages
- A category filter address (`/products?category=<key>`, `/urunler?category=<key>` in Turkish) is an indexable page. It has its own title, description, heading and an optional intro text that shows on page 1 only.
- The page has a self canonical address and language alternates. A category with no products is sent as `noindex`.
- Product and category pages show a visible breadcrumb. It matches the `BreadcrumbList` structured data and replaces the back link.
- The Products menu in the header lists the categories.
- The work area cards on the home page link to their category.
- The sitemap lists one entry per category and language, for categories that have products.
- The panel has four optional fields per category and language: search title, search description, heading and intro. Each is filled in both languages or in neither. The application areas screen has a select for the linked category.

# Site discovery
- A plain-text site guide lists the eight public pages in Turkish and English. Its addresses follow `SITE_URL`. It is not part of the sitemap.
- The public contact form carries name and description hints for tools that read forms in the page.
- The demo seed adds four social links (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube) for the footer. They point to accounts the project does not own.
- In demo mode the `Organization` data has no `sameAs` list and no contact point, because the profiles and contact details are not the brand's own. Outside demo mode `sameAs` lists the saved social links, and the logo falls back to the app icon when none is set.

# Performance
- The first photo of each page loads at once with high fetch priority.
- The footer logo is a smaller WebP file and loads lazily. Three work area photos are lighter.
- The watermark behind section titles is drawn in CSS from the title text, so the text appears once in the HTML.
- Optimized images are cached for 31 days. The `/brand`, `/icons` and `/demo` folders send a one day cache with a week of background revalidation.
- The public client bundle no longer includes the translation runtime. The language links in the menu use a list of page addresses.
- Photos use a plain `img` element.
- Local measurement for `/tr` with Lighthouse: performance 93 to 95, mobile largest contentful paint 3259 ms to 3003 ms, page weight 490.8 KB to 472.4 KB. These numbers come from one local setup and are not a guarantee for other machines or for the live site.

# Upgrade note
- On a demo site, run `pnpm db:seed:demo` after deploying. It writes the category page texts, the links from work areas to categories and the demo social links. Without it, the category pages show no own text and the area cards keep their old address.

# Known limits
- The live site stays `noindex` until 1.0.0.
- There is no blog yet.
- The Industrial Sites work area has no matching category, so its card has no category link.

## Technical notes
- Migrations (1), applied by the build with `prisma migrate deploy`:
  - `20261103080000_category_seo_area_link`: eight text columns on `category` (empty by default) and a nullable `categoryId` on `applicationArea`, with a foreign key that sets the value to null when the category is deleted.
- `pnpm db:seed:demo` is not part of the build. Run it separately on a demo site.
- `next.config.ts` sets `images.minimumCacheTTL` to 2678400 seconds and adds cache headers for `/brand`, `/icons` and `/demo`. A replaced demo photo keeps its address, so it shows once the optimizer entry has expired.
- The plain-text site guide is a route handler without input. It is on the public allowlist of the admin guard check.
- `lighthouse` is updated to 13.6.0.
